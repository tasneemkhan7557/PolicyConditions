
Privacy Policy for MissYou

Developer: Sam Summer
Effective Date: November 21, 2026
Sam Summer ("we", "our", or "us") operates the MissYou mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.
1. No GPS or Real-Time Location Tracking
MissYou does not collect, track, or share your real-time GPS location. The Service strictly tracks time, dates, and durations (such as how much time has passed since partners were last together) based on the dates and time zones you input or select.
2. Information We Collect
Depending on how you use the app, we may collect the following limited information:
• Time & Milestone Data: Dates, timestamps, time zones, and custom labels/notes you enter to calculate the time passed or remaining.
• Account & Pairing Information (if applicable): If your app syncs between two devices, we collect basic identifiers (such as an email address, username, or unique pairing code) solely to link you with your chosen partner.
• Usage & Device Data: Basic, non-personal technical information (such as device model, operating system version, and crash logs) to help us maintain and improve app stability.
3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information strictly to:
• Calculate and display the time passed or countdowns between you and your partner.
• Sync milestone updates and send optional reminders or notifications.
• Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.
4. Sharing of Information
• Partner Syncing: Any shared timers, dates, or statuses are visible only to the specific partner you have explicitly paired with inside the app.
• No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or data brokers.
• Service Providers: We may use trusted third-party services (such as cloud databases or analytics providers) solely to operate the app, and they are obligated not to disclose or use your data for any other purpose.
5. Data Retention and Deletion
We retain your saved dates and account data only for as long as you use the Service. You may clear your data, unpair from a partner, or delete your account at any time through the app settings or by contacting us via email.
6. Children's Privacy
Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
